## Common things

**"Sign in failed"** - use the same email and password you bought with at
clickindicatorsmod.com. Your account is not your Geometry Dash account.

**"Signed in on another device"** - your licence works on one device at a time.
Signing in inside the game on another PC moves it there and signs the old one
out. To bring it back, just sign in again on this PC; nothing needs doing on the
website. If it keeps moving on its own, someone else has your password - change
it on the site.

**"Too many attempts"** - too many wrong passwords from this connection. It
clears on its own; a correct password is never blocked.

**No macros for a level** - most levels have none. Record your own: hit Record
in the pause menu, slow the game down, play through, then Save.

**Something looks wrong** - turn on *Debug logging* in the mod's Troubleshooting
settings, reproduce it, and send the Geode log with your bug report.
