CLICK INDICATORS
Thank you for your purchase.

Your license key is in this order's confirmation email.


INSTALLING
1. Install Geode if you do not have it yet:  https://geode-sdk.org
2. Open Geometry Dash, go to the Geode menu, and search for "Click Indicators".
3. Install it and restart the game.

The mod is free to download - it stays locked until you activate your key.


ACTIVATING
1. On the Geometry Dash main menu, click the Click Indicators button.
2. Paste your license key.
3. Press Activate.

That is it. The button now appears in the pause menu during a level, and the
guide turns on.


GOOD TO KNOW
- One key works on up to 5 installs. Reinstalling can use one up; if you run
  out, open a ticket and it will be reset.
- The mod checks your key online about once a day and keeps working offline
  for two weeks between checks.
- Updates are free.
