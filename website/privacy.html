Privacy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Click Indicators is a Geometry Dash mod sold on this site by MSH Tech, LLC (California, USA). This page says exactly what data it holds, why, and how to get rid of it. It's short because there isn't much.

What the account holds

DataWhy
Your email addressIt's your login, and it's how you'd be contacted about your purchase.
Your password, hashedStored as a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash with a random salt. The password itself is never written down anywhere, so nobody here can read it or tell you what it is.
Whether you've bought itDecides whether the mod runs and whether you can download it.
A purchase recordThe Stripe payment id, the amount, and the date. Kept for tax and accounting.
One device recordA random id the mod generates for your install (something like GD-a1d24cba7feb), plus when it last checked in. This is what enforces one device at a time. It is not a hardware id and it says nothing about your PC.
Your IP address, brieflyOnly as a counter against repeated failed sign-ins, so nobody can sit there guessing passwords. It isn't linked to your account or used for anything else.

Payment

Stripe handles the payment. Your card details go to them, never to this site — they aren't received here and couldn't be stored here even by accident. What comes back is a payment id, the amount, and confirmation it went through. Stripe has their own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

What the mod does over the internet

Worth knowing, since it isn't obvious from inside the game:

  • It contacts clickindicators.com to check your licence — when the game starts, and at most once every half hour while you play. It sends only your device token. If it can't reach the server it keeps working offline for up to 14 days. Some networks block that address outright, so if it gets no answer at all it tries the same server by two other names — clickindicatorsmod.com and clickindicators-api.msmithbh9.workers.dev — which are the same service, not anyone else's.
  • It contacts hyperbolus.net when you download macros for a level, and sends the level id so it knows what to look for. That's a third-party site with its own policies.
  • Nothing else. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no ads. Your gameplay, your macros and your settings stay on your PC.

This site

One cookie, ci_session, which keeps you signed in. It's essential — sign-in can't work without it — so there's no cookie banner to click. No analytics or tracking scripts of any kind. Cloudflare Turnstile runs on the sign-in form to keep bots out.

How long it's kept

Your account stays until you ask for it to go. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, and device records disappear when that install signs out or gets replaced. Purchase records are kept even after an account is deleted, because tax rules require it — those are reduced to the payment id, amount and date, with no email attached.

Deleting your account

Email support@clickindicatorsmod.com from the address on the account and say you want it deleted. Your account, sessions and device records get removed. Deleting the account means losing access to the mod, and since purchases aren't refundable that isn't reversible — so it's worth being sure.

You can also ask for a copy of what's held on you, at the same address.

Nobody else gets it

Your data isn't sold, rented, or handed to anyone for advertising. The only third parties involved are Stripe (payment) and Cloudflare (hosting the site and database, and the bot check). Both are processors acting on instructions, not customers buying a list.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
