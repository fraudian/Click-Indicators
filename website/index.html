Does it work on the current version of Geometry Dash?

It's built for 2.2 and runs through Geode, so it keeps working as long as Geode does. When GD updates and Geode catches up, the mod gets rebuilt against it and the new build appears in your account.

Does it conflict with Mega Hack?

No. They're both Geode mods and they load alongside each other. Click Indicators only draws an overlay and reads your input timing.

Will this get me banned, or flag a level submission?

It doesn't touch your inputs, your physics, or the game's servers. It draws on top of the screen and grades what you already pressed. It's a practice tool, not something to run on a verification attempt. If you're submitting a run, turn it off.

Is a level really yours if you learned it with this?

You still have to hit every input yourself, at speed, without the mod on. What it removes is the guessing about when. That's the same thing a practice-mode checkpoint or a slowed-down clip does, drawn where you're already looking.
