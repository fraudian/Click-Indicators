# Click Indicators

Shows you when to click.

Reads macro files from players who beat the level and draws a cue that resolves
on the frame the macro pressed. Turns bright green when you are on target.

## Cues

- **Ring** - collapses onto your icon. Most precise, start here.
- **Classic** - scrolling line and window. Shows how wide the window is.
- **Converge** - two lines meet on you, doesn't cover your icon.
- **Pulse** - just the reticle brightening. Minimal.
- **Highway** - Guitar Hero lane in the corner. Notes fall to a bar, press as each
  lands, holds are long notes. The only style that shows several clicks ahead at once.

## It learns your timing

Everyone clicks early or late by a bit. Calibration measures yours over
about 40 clicks and shifts the cue to match. Holds still during an attempt so
the target doesn't move while you're trying to hit it.

## Macros

Download per level from inside the game, load your own file, or record your own
run. Recording is frame-based - slow the game down, play through once, and the
saved macro lines up at full speed.

Reads Silicate, GDR2, GDR, zBot, Mega Hack, ReplayBot, TASBOT, Echo, DDHOR, xBot, JSON.

**Safe mode** is on by default. Nothing you do in a level counts while it's on -
no best percentage, no completion, no stars, orbs, diamonds or coins, no
attempts, nothing to the leaderboard. Turn it off in settings if you want runs
to count. The mod asks first, and the attempt you're already in still won't count.

## Notes

- This is a practice tool. It never presses anything for you, it only shows you
  when to press. What you do with that is up to your hands.
- Requires an account. Buy once at **clickindicatorsmod.com**, then sign in from
  the main menu button.
- Works in every gamemode, toggleable per gamemode.
